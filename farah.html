My Juliet, Farah

Verona of my heart — August 4th
My dearest Farah,

If Juliet had a wall in Verona where strangers left her their hearts on paper, then let this page be my wall, and you the only one I have ever wanted to write to. Twenty-three years ago today, the world got its best idea yet. So before anything else — happy birthday, my love.

I keep thinking about how easy it is to love you in the small, ordinary moments, the ones nobody would think to put in a movie. So today I wanted to write a few of them down, the way Sophie collected letters that had waited fifty years to be answered — except I don't want to wait. I want to tell you now, while it's still today, while it's still your day.

  • The way you play rondo like you invented it — always one step ahead, laughing every time the ball finds you again. ⚽
  • Our kayak drifting further than either of us planned, both of us pretending we knew where we were going. 🛶
  • Me, standing in the heaviest rain of the year, eating ice cream like it was the most reasonable decision in the world, just to make you laugh. 🍨
  • The night we watched Letters to Juliet together, apart in two different homes, and somehow it still felt like the same room. 🎬

That's the part of us I love most, Farah — that distance never really was distance. A screen, a few hundred kilometers, an ordinary Tuesday — none of it has ever been strong enough to make me feel far from you. If Claire could find her fifty-year-old love again, I figure I'm allowed to be a little bit hopeful about ours too.

So here is my letter, left for you the way Verona's wall keeps its promises: I am so glad it's your birthday. I am so glad it's you. Whatever this next year brings, I hope it's full of rondo mornings, reckless kayaks, ice cream in bad weather, and a hundred more movie nights — together in the same room, or in two, it never really mattered.

Happy birthday, my Juliet.

All my love, always,
Yours
"Whatever, wherever, don't ever lose courage." — Letters to Juliet
